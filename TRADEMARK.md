# Botsivo Video trademark policy

**Botsivo Video** is a fork of [Concat](https://github.com/jub0t/Concat). The
source code remains free software under AGPL-3.0-or-later. The **Botsivo** and
**Botsivo Video** names and logos are not part of that grant.

Upstream Concat keeps its own name and logo; do not present this fork as
official Concat. See Concat's [TRADEMARK.md](https://github.com/jub0t/Concat/blob/main/TRADEMARK.md).

## The marks

- The word marks **Botsivo** and **Botsivo Video**.
- The Botsivo logo and its variants in [`assets/`](assets/).

## What you may do without asking

- Nominative use: "compatible with Botsivo Video", "a fork of Botsivo Video".
- Redistribute unmodified official Botsivo Video builds under that name.
- Write about the project (reviews, tutorials, screenshots).

## What needs a different name

If you distribute a **modified** Botsivo Video, give it your own name and icon
before you ship.

## Asking

Trademark questions: contact the Botsivo maintainers via the GitHub organization
[botsivo-crm](https://github.com/botsivo-crm).
